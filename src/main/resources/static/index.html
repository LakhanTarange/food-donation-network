About FoodShareConnect

FoodShareConnect is a food waste management and donation network that connects people who have surplus food with people who need it.


Our mission is to reduce food wastage, support communities, and encourage responsible food donation.


Through our Donate Food, Find Food and Chat features, donors and receivers can coordinate food donations.
